Macao's Diversification Pathways — AHP-Based Industry Evaluation
Regional Economics Course · Chongqing University · 2023–2024 · Team Leader & Presenter

This project evaluated the role of the gaming industry as Macao's economic pillar and proposed a structured diversification strategy. Using AHP, we built a multi-dimensional evaluation framework to quantify the positive and negative impacts of gaming — across economic, social, cultural, and environmental dimensions — and reached a net-positive impact conclusion. The project maps closely to the analytical work required in ratings: understanding industry structure, policy environment, risk concentration, and economic sustainability.

Key Findings — Gaming Industry Impact Assessment
Positive contributions
GDP concentration
Gaming tax exceeds 80% of government revenue; industry once contributed ~60% of GDP.
Employment multiplier
Gaming + tourism together employ over 50% of the workforce; strong indirect effect on hospitality and retail.
International positioning
Gaming revenue surpassed Las Vegas in 2006; elevated global brand recognition drives tourist inflows.
Risks & constraints
Single-industry concentration risk
Over-reliance on gaming creates severe vulnerability to regulatory changes, geopolitical events, and demand shocks (e.g. COVID-19 impact).
Social externalities
Problem gambling, income inequality, and social cohesion pressures require active policy management.
Diversification bottleneck
Human capital, land, and capital are heavily locked into gaming; switching costs for alternative industries are high.
